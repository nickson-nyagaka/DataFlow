## 2. Semester Logic by Indicator Family

As identified in Lead L-15, semester logic differs substantially across indicators. The table below documents the exact semester logic implemented in the notebook.

| Indicator Family | Output Column(s) | Logic Type | Semester Definition | Legacy Quirks / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HTS (General) | `HTS_SEM` | AND / OR with Enrolment Date threshold | `((HTS_Q1 > 0) & (HTS_Q2 > 0)) | ((HTS_Q2 > 0) & (enrolled_month >= 7))` | Code tests month >= 7 (July); comment states April / month >= 10. Year is completely ignored (OQ-06). |
| HTS (Any) | `HTS_SEM_ANY / HTS_Tested` | OR (Union) | `(HTS_Q1 > 0) | (HTS_Q2 > 0)` | Client tested in either quarter. Used in Cell 84 disaggregated pivot. |
| PrEP | `PREP_SEM` | OR (Union) | `(PREP_Q1 + PREP_Q2) >= 1` | Client active on PrEP (initiated, restart, switched, refill) in either Q1 or Q2. |
| STI Screening | `STI_SCREENED_SEM` | OR (Union) | `(STI_Screened_Q1 + STI_Screened_Q2) >= 1` | Client screened for STIs in either Q1 or Q2. |
| STI Diagnosis | `STI_DIAGNOSED_SEM` | OR (Union) | `(STI_Diagnosed_Q1 + STI_Diagnosed_Q2) >= 1` | Client diagnosed with STI in either Q1 or Q2. |
| STI Treatment | `STI_TREATED_SEM` | OR (Union) | `(STI_Treated_Q1 + STI_Treated_Q2) >= 1` | Client treated for STI in either Q1 or Q2. |
| TB Screening | `TB_SCREENED_SEM` | OR (Union) | `(TB_Screened_Q1 + TB_Screened_Q2) >= 1` | Client screened for TB in either Q1 or Q2. |
| Peer Education | `PeerEducation_SEM` | OR (Union) | `(PeerEd_Q1 + PeerEd_Q2) >= 1` | Client received peer education in either Q1 or Q2. |
| Condoms Received (Client) | `ReceivedCondom_SEM` | OR (Union) | `(ReceivedCondom_Q1 + ReceivedCondom_Q2) >= 1` | Client received condoms (>0) in either Q1 or Q2. |
| Condoms Total (Commodity) | `total_condoms_SEM` | Sum (Commodity total) | `total_condoms_Q1 + total_condoms_Q2` | Total condoms distributed across all visits in Q1 + Q2. |
| NSP Received (Client, PWID) | `NSP_SEM` | OR (Union) | `(NSP_Q1 + NSP_Q2) >= 1` | PWID received needles/syringes (>0) in either Q1 or Q2. |
| NSP Total (Commodity, PWID) | `total_nsp_SEM` | Sum (Commodity total) | `total_nsp_Q1 + total_nsp_Q2` | Total needles/syringes distributed in Q1 + Q2. Omitted in Cell 82 due to uncomputed column bug (NI-01). |
| Defined Package | `DefinedPackage_SEM` | OR (Union) | `(DefinedPackage_Q1 + DefinedPackage_Q2) >= 1` | Client met Defined Package requirements in either Q1 or Q2. |
| ART / New on ART | `New_on_ART, CurrentArt` | No semester aggregation | `NONE (Q1 and Q2 only)` | Legacy code calculates Q1 and Q2 only; no New_on_ART_SEM or CurrentArt_SEM exists in summary tables or Excel exports. |
